Privacy Policy

This is a private, single-user personal assistant app. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.

What it connects to

With the owner's permission, the app can read data from the owner's own Google Calendar (read-only) and the owner's own WHOOP account (recovery and workouts, read-only). It shows that data to the owner and never writes anything back to those services.

What is stored

The app stores the sign-in tokens needed to keep those connections working, in the owner's own private database. Calendar and WHOOP data are fetched when needed and kept only briefly in memory for speed.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone else, and is not used for advertising or analytics.

Disconnecting

The owner can disconnect either service at any time from within the app, which deletes the stored tokens. Access can also be revoked from the Google or WHOOP account settings.
